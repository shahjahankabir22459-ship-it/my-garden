✂️ Essential Care Guide

Nurturing Healthy Plants

Core principles to keep balcony flora flourishing, healthy, and bug-free year round.

1. Sunlight Management

Flowering plants require 4–8 hours of direct light. Arrange shade-loving species behind taller creepers to safeguard delicate foliage from scorching midday heat.

2. Precision Watering

Water deeply during early mornings so moisture penetrates the root zone before evaporating. Avoid overwatering to prevent root rot and fungus growth.

3. Soil & Organic Fertilizer

Utilize well-draining potting soil mixed with composted cow manure or organic leaf mold. Aerate upper soil layers monthly for proper root respiration.

4. Pruning & Pest Defense

Regularly deadhead spent blossoms and trim dry stems. Inspect underside of leaves weekly for pests, using organic neem oil spray when needed.

5. Air Circulation

Ensure proper airflow between plants by spacing them adequately. Good ventilation reduces humidity buildup, discourages fungal infections, and strengthens stems.

6. Climate & Seasonal Care

Adjust care routines according to seasonal changes. Protect sensitive plants from frost with covers, and provide extra hydration during peak summer heat.
