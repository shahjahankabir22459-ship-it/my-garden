1. Sunlight ☀️


Sunlight is essential for the healthy growth of plants. Most flowering and fruit-bearing plants need about 4-8 hours of sunlight every day. However, someplants grow well in partial shade. We should place each plant in a suitable place according to its sunlight requirements. Too much direct sunlight can damage. delicate plants, while too little sunlight can make them weak. Therefore, proper sunlight helps plants grow healthy and produce beautiful flowers and fruits.

2. Watering 💧


Water is another important requirement for a garden. Plants need water to grow and remain fresh. We should water our plants regularly, especially during hot and dry weather. However, we should not water them too much because excess water can cause root rot. The amount of water depends on the type of plant, the size of its pot, and the weather. Early morning is generally a good time to water plants because thewater can reach the roots before the day becomes too hot.

3. Frtilizer and Soil 🌱


Fertilizer and good soil are necessary for the proper growth of plants. We should use fertile, well-drained soil that contains the nutrients plants need. Organic fertilizers such as compost and decomposed cow dung, can improve soil quality and help plants grow well. We should apply fertilizer in the right amount because too much ertilizer can damage the roots. We should also loosen the soil occasionally to improve air circulation. Healthy soil helps plants develop strong roots, green leaves, and beautiful flowers.

4. Pruning and protecting ✂️


Pruning and protecting plants are also important parts of garden care. We should remove dead leaves, dry branches, and faded flowers regularly. This helps plants remain healthy and look beautiful. We should also check our plants for insects and diseases. If we find any problems, we should take appropriate measures to protect them. Removing weeds is equally important because weeds compete with garden plants for water, sunlight, and nutrients. With regular care and attention, we caneep our garden clean, healthy, and attractive.
